## Question

What user-visible behavior should the Thin Reference Shell use when the on-device model, Claude authentication, network, required model capability, or a tool is unavailable, interrupted, or fails? Decide which states disable a Model Strategy, which may retry or fall back without violating always-local-first privacy, how partial results and cart previews behave, and what Model Trace records. Base the decision on the verified provider constraints and the hybrid-boundary prototype rather than silently routing around failures.
